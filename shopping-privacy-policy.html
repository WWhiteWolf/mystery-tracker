Shopping — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

This privacy policy covers the Shopping List app only. On the App Store it is listed as Shopping. The name under the icon is Shopping.

Shopping List collects no personal data of any kind. There are no user accounts, no sign-in, and no analytics or tracking of any kind built into the app. Nothing the app stores is ever sent anywhere automatically. Everything you enter into the app — your inventory and the things you need to buy — lives only on your own phone, in the app's own local storage.

Shopping List does not share, sell, or transmit any information to any third party. There is no advertising in the app.

If you have questions about this privacy policy, you can reach the developer at jojoMurphy@tuta.com.
