Mystery Clues Track Sheet — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 24, 2026

This privacy policy covers the Mystery Clues Track Sheet app only.

Mystery Clues Track Sheet collects no personal data of any kind. There are no user accounts, no sign-in, and no analytics or tracking of any kind built into the app. Nothing the app stores is ever sent anywhere automatically. Everything you enter into the app — your player names, your card deck, your marks and notes during a game — lives only on your own phone, in the app's own local storage.

The one feature that connects to anything outside your phone is the app's Feedback option. If you choose to use it, the app opens your phone's own Mail app with a pre-written draft addressed to the app's developer. Nothing is sent unless you choose to send that email yourself, and the only content in that email is whatever you typed into the feedback form, plus the rating you chose.

Mystery Clues Track Sheet does not share, sell, or transmit any information to any third party. There is no advertising in the app.

If you have questions about this privacy policy, you can reach the developer at jojoMurphy@tuta.com.
